# Project: Conducting a Security Audit

## Objective
The objective of this project was to perform a comprehensive security audit for a fictional company, "Botium Toys." The goal was to identify gaps in existing security controls, assess compliance with major industry regulations, and develop a prioritized remediation plan to address the most critical risks.

---

## Process & Findings
The audit was conducted using a systematic, checklist-based approach to evaluate the company's security posture:

* **1. [cite_start]Control Assessment:** I completed a detailed controls checklist to inventory the company's existing security measures[cite: 760, 764]. [cite_start]The audit revealed critical gaps, including the absence of an Intrusion Detection System (IDS), formal disaster recovery plans, and data backups[cite: 794, 798, 799].

* **2. [cite_start]Compliance Evaluation:** I assessed the company's adherence to best practices for key regulations, including the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and System and Organization Controls (SOC)[cite: 814, 826, 842].

* **3. [cite_start]Remediation Strategy:** Based on the findings, I developed a prioritized remediation plan to address the most critical vulnerabilities[cite: 859]. [cite_start]High-priority recommendations included implementing the principles of Least Privilege and Separation of Duties, deploying encryption for all cardholder data, and establishing a robust data backup system[cite: 861, 866, 869].

---

## Skills Demonstrated
* **Security Auditing & Control Assessment:** Systematically evaluating an organization's security controls against established criteria.
* **Compliance Management:** Applying knowledge of PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC standards to identify compliance gaps.
* **Risk Analysis & Prioritization:** Identifying vulnerabilities and prioritizing them based on their potential impact on the organization.
* **Remediation Planning:** Developing clear, actionable strategies to mitigate identified security risks.
* **Technical Writing & Reporting:** Communicating complex security findings and recommendations in a clear and concise report.
